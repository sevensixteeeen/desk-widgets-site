Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 (event creation added)

What the app accesses

If you connect Google Calendar, Desk Widgets reads the list of calendars shown in your Google Calendar and the events on them (title, start time, and whether they're all-day). It can also add new events that you create yourself in the calendar widget. It never changes or deletes existing events.

How it's used

Events are shown only in the calendar widget on your own desktop. New events are sent to your own Google Calendar only when you click Add. They are fetched directly from Google to your computer and are not saved, sent to any other server, sold, or shared with anyone.

What's stored

A Google sign-in token is saved on your computer, in the app's data folder, so you don't have to sign in each time. It never leaves your computer except when sent to Google to refresh access.

The weather widget sends the city name you type to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the forecast. Nothing else about you is sent.

Google API Services

Desk Widgets' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes the saved token.
